Grooming Waiver and Release

Version Effective

By booking with Swag Cuts, you acknowledge and accept the following grooming waiver and release.

Health and behavior disclosure

You confirm your pet is healthy enough for grooming and will inform us about medical conditions, allergies, aggressive behavior, or prior grooming incidents.

Handling and risk

Grooming involves the use of clippers, shears, dryers, and restraint tools. While we take reasonable care, you acknowledge that nicks, clipper irritation, or stress reactions may occur.

Matting and coat conditions

Severe matting may require additional time or a short shave for humane grooming. You authorize us to use professional judgment for your pet's comfort.

Emergency care

If we believe your pet needs urgent care, we will attempt to contact you. You authorize us to seek veterinary care at your expense if necessary.

Release

You release Swag Cuts and its staff from liability for conditions that arise despite reasonable care, including pre-existing health issues, stress-related incidents, or minor grooming-related injuries.
