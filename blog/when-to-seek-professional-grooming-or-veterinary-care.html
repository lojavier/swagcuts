When to seek professional grooming or veterinary care

February 9, 2026 2 min read

Many pet issues can be solved with a professional groom, but some symptoms need medical care first. Use this quick guide to choose the right next step.

Book a groomer when you notice

  • Coat matting, tangles, or heavy shedding.
  • Nails that are long, sharp, or clicking on the floor.
  • Mild odor, dull coat, or dry skin that improves with baths.
  • Ear wax build-up without pain or redness.

Call the vet when you see

  • Open sores, bleeding, or broken skin.
  • Persistent itching, hot spots, or hair loss patches.
  • Swelling, limping, or sudden behavior changes.
  • Ear discharge, head shaking, or foul odor in the ears.

Sometimes you need both

  • Skin irritation that needs treatment and a gentle groom.
  • Parasites or flea dirt that require medical guidance.
  • Post-surgery coats that need careful maintenance.

Choose fast with a simple check

If your pet seems in pain, bleeding, or not acting like themselves, contact a veterinarian first. If the concern is coat, nails, or hygiene, start with a groomer and share your notes.
