How to spot health problems during pet grooming

February 2, 2026 1 min read

Grooming time is a perfect moment to check in on your pet. Look for these signs and share them with your vet or groomer.

Skin and coat changes

  • Redness, flaky patches, or hot spots.
  • Lumps, bumps, or scabs under the fur.
  • Sudden hair loss or thinning areas.

Ears, eyes, and mouth

  • Ear odor, discharge, or head shaking.
  • Cloudy eyes or excessive tearing.
  • Bad breath, red gums, or broken teeth.

Paws and movement

  • Cracked paw pads or constant licking.
  • Stiff joints or limping after rest.
  • Overgrown nails that change posture.

When to call a vet

If you notice pain, swelling, or major behavior changes, contact your veterinarian right away.
