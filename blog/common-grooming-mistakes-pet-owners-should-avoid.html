Common grooming mistakes pet owners should avoid

February 7, 2026 2 min read

Home grooming is helpful, but a few missteps can cause discomfort or damage. Use these tips to keep grooming safe and effective.

Skipping regular brush-outs

Matting builds quickly on long coats. Brush a little every few days and schedule professional grooms before tangles tighten.

Using the wrong tools

  • Choose brushes based on coat type, not brand hype.
  • Avoid pulling combs through tight mats.
  • Replace dull nail tools to prevent splitting.

Bathing too often

Over-bathing strips oils and dries skin. Follow your groomer or vet guidelines, and use gentle formulas.

Trimming too close at home

Home trims should be small touchups. Leave major cuts to a groomer to avoid uneven lines or accidental nicks.

Forgetting ears, nails, and paw pads

Small areas make a big difference. Regular nail trims, ear checks, and paw pad cleanups keep pets comfortable.
