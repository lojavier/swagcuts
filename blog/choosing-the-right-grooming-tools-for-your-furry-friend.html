Choosing the right grooming tools for your furry friend

February 3, 2026 1 min read

Every coat type needs a different toolkit. Use this guide to build a simple setup that keeps grooming easy and safe.

Brushes by coat type

  • Slicker brush for curly or long coats.
  • Bristle brush for short, smooth coats.
  • Undercoat rake for double-coated breeds.

Nail care basics

Trim a little at a time and keep treats nearby. Ask a groomer if you are unsure about nail length.

Bathing and drying

  • Use a gentle pet shampoo, not human products.
  • Dry thoroughly to prevent skin irritation.
  • Keep towels and a detangling comb within reach.

When to ask for help

If your pet has matting, anxious behavior, or skin concerns, a professional groomer can help prevent discomfort.
