Top signs your pet needs to visit the veterinarian

February 5, 2026 1 min read

Some symptoms require quick medical attention. If you notice any of these warning signs, contact your veterinarian right away.

Urgent warning signs

  • Difficulty breathing, collapse, or extreme lethargy.
  • Persistent vomiting or diarrhea.
  • Uncontrolled bleeding or severe swelling.
  • Seizures or sudden loss of balance.

Ongoing issues that need a checkup

  • Weight loss or sudden appetite changes.
  • Excessive scratching, chewing, or hot spots.
  • Bad breath, red gums, or drooling.
  • Frequent accidents or changes in bathroom habits.

What to share with your vet

Bring notes about symptoms, timing, and behavior changes. Photos can help if a skin issue comes and goes.
