Shedding season essentials

January 10, 2026 1 min read

When shedding ramps up, a steady routine keeps coats healthy and homes clean.

Weekly routine

  • Brush three times a week to release loose fur.
  • Use a de-shedding tool only as recommended for your coat type.
  • Schedule a bath every four to six weeks.

Grooming add-ons

Add a de-shed treatment to your appointment for the best results.
