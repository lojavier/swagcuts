Understanding routine veterinary checkups for pets

February 6, 2026 2 min read

Regular checkups help catch issues early and keep pets healthy. Here is what to expect and how to prepare for each visit.

How often should pets visit the vet?

  • Puppies and kittens: every 3 to 4 weeks during vaccine series.
  • Healthy adults: once per year for wellness exams.
  • Senior pets: every 6 months for proactive screening.

What your vet checks

  • Weight, body condition, and hydration.
  • Eyes, ears, teeth, skin, and coat.
  • Heart, lungs, joints, and mobility.
  • Parasite prevention and vaccine status.

How to prepare for the appointment

  • Bring vaccination records and current medications.
  • Write down questions about behavior, appetite, or energy.
  • Note any changes in coat, skin, or grooming needs.

Where grooming fits in

Grooming appointments are a great time to monitor skin and coat changes between vet visits. Share any concerns with your groomer and veterinarian.
