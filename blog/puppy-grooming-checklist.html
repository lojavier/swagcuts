Puppy grooming checklist

January 15, 2026 1 min read

Use this checklist to help your puppy feel safe and confident before their first grooming appointment.

Before the visit

  • Schedule a short play session to burn off energy.
  • Handle paws, ears, and tail gently at home.
  • Bring vaccination records and any vet notes.

What to bring

  • Favorite treats for positive reinforcement.
  • A leash or carrier for safe transfer.
  • Notes about allergies or sensitivities.
