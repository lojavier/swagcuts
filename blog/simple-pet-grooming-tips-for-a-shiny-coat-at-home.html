Simple pet grooming tips for a shiny coat at home

February 1, 2026 1 min read

Daily habits make a big difference. Use these at-home tips to keep coats healthy between professional appointments.

Brush on a schedule

  • Short coats: 1 to 2 times a week.
  • Medium coats: 3 times a week.
  • Long coats: light brushing every day.

Bathing basics

Use lukewarm water, pet-safe shampoo, and rinse thoroughly. Follow with a towel dry and gentle brushing.

Nutrition supports the shine

Healthy coats start with balanced meals and plenty of water. Ask your groomer or vet if your pet needs a diet change.

Keep up with nails and paws

Trim nails regularly and check paw pads for dryness or debris after walks.
