Our story

Swag Cuts started as a mobile grooming service and grew into a local studio that blends spa-level care with modern convenience. We focus on comfort, safety, and long-term coat health.

Our groomers train on breed-specific styles, skin sensitivities, and positive handling to keep every visit calm and predictable.

What makes us different

  • One-on-one grooming for anxious pups.
  • Transparent pricing based on coat condition.
  • Clean, fragrance-light products.
  • Optional add-ons for senior care and de-shedding.
