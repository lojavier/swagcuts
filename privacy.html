Privacy Policy

Version Effective

This Privacy Policy explains how Swag Cuts collects, uses, and shares information when you visit our website or request grooming services. We are based in Oakley, California.

Information we collect

  • Contact details such as name, email, phone, and address.
  • Pet details like breed, size, age, health notes, and grooming preferences.
  • Booking details, service selections, and communication history.
  • Payment information processed through our payment provider.
  • Website usage data, including device, browser, and pages visited.

How we use information

  • Confirm and manage appointments.
  • Provide service updates, reminders, and receipts.
  • Improve our services, products, and website experience.
  • Send promotional messages only if you opt in.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce policies.

How we share information

We share information with service providers who help us operate the business, such as payment processors, scheduling tools, and notification services. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.

Data retention

We keep information only as long as needed to fulfill services, maintain records, and meet legal requirements.

Your choices

  • Opt out of marketing messages at any time.
  • Request access, correction, or deletion of your data.
  • Manage cookie preferences through the privacy controls.

California privacy rights

California residents have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by the CPRA.

  • Request access to the categories and specific pieces of personal information we collect.
  • Request deletion of personal information, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Submit requests by emailing hello@swagcuts.com. We may need to verify your identity and you may use an authorized agent. We do not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

We do not sell personal information. If we use marketing cookies that could be considered sharing, you can opt out using the Privacy controls or by contacting us.

Contact

Email privacy questions to hello@swagcuts.com.
