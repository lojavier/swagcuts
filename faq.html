Frequently asked questions

Answers to common grooming and booking questions in Oakley.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking 1 to 2 weeks in advance for the best availability.

Do you groom puppies?

Yes. We offer gentle puppy introductions with shorter appointment times.

What vaccinations are required?

Dogs must be current on core vaccinations. Contact us if you need a list.

How do you handle matting?

We use humane techniques and will discuss options before proceeding.

Can I stay during the groom?

For safety, we ask owners to return at pickup time unless pre-arranged.

Do you offer mobile grooming?

Yes, limited mobile appointments are available in Oakley and nearby areas.

Still have questions?

We are happy to help before you book.
